WHO WE ARE
About PayChex
PayChex International Marketing Limited is a wholly Nigerian-owned engineering, 
construction and technology services company incorporated in 2008. Over more than 
a decade and a half, we have grown from a service enterprise into an integrated 
solutions provider trusted by government institutions, development partners and 
private-sector clients to deliver complex projects from concept to commissioning. We 
operate at the intersection of civil and structural engineering, power infrastructure, 
technology services and strategic project coordination — giving our clients a single, 
accountable partner across the entire project lifecycle. Our work is led by a 
multidisciplinary team of engineers, project managers, technologists and 
administrators whose combined experience spans construction, telecommunications, 
power systems, public-sector programme delivery and large-scale stakeholder 
engagement. With operating offices in Abuja, Lagos and Kaduna and a nationwide 
network of technical teams and field agents, PayChex has the reach, the systems 
and the discipline to execute projects of national scale while holding firmly to 
international standards of quality, safety and environmental responsibility.

Turnkey & EPC Construction
We deliver complete, ready-to-use facilities under Engineering, 
Procurement and Construction (EPC) contracts — assuming single-point 
responsibility for the entire project so our clients receive a finished, 
commissioned asset and a single line of accountability.
• End-to-end delivery: feasibility, design, engineering, procurement, 
construction, testing and commissioning under one contract.
• Single-point accountability that removes interface risk between 
designers, suppliers and contractors.
• Fixed-scope, fixed-programme delivery models with transparent 
milestone reporting.
• Integrated handover packages including as-built documentation, O&M 
manuals and operator training.
What you get   Fully commissioned, fit-for-purpose facilities delivered on schedule 
and within budget.
Civil Engineering & Building Construction
From site preparation to structural completion, we construct residential, 
commercial, institutional and industrial buildings to exacting design 
specifications and quality standards.
• Site surveys, land clearing, earthworks and full site preparation.
• Reinforced-concrete and structural works for residential, commercial 
and industrial buildings.
• Roads, drainage and ancillary civil infrastructure.
• Renovation, remodelling and structural upgrades that modernise 
existing assets while preserving their character.
What you get   Durable, code-compliant structures built to design intent and quality 
benchmarks.

Telecommunications & Connectivity Infrastructure
We Design, fabricating and install telecommunications towers to global standards, We also deliver the end-to-end deployment of Fibre Optic last 
mile infrastructure delivering connectivity and hosting as a licensed Internet Service Provider .
Telecommunications Towers & Masts  
Fabrication & Installation
We design, fabricate and install telecommunications towers and masts 
engineered to international structural standards, providing the physical 
backbone for mobile, broadband and broadcast networks across the 
country.
• Fabrication of self-supporting lattice towers, guyed masts, monopoles 
and rooftop structures.
• Structural engineering and analysis to global standards (e.g. TIA-222 / 
EIA) for wind, load and seismic resilience.
• Hot-dip galvanising and protective coating for long-term corrosion 
resistance and durability.
• Site survey, foundation works, erection, antenna mounting and 
commissioning by certified riggers.
What you get   Durable, standards-compliant tower infrastructure ready to carry 
mission-critical networks.
Internet Service Provision  (Licensed ISP)
As a licensed Internet Service Provider, we deliver reliable, high-speed 
connectivity for homes, businesses and institutions — from fibre and 
dedicated bandwidth to fully managed enterprise networks.
• High-speed fibre-optic internet with dedicated, guaranteed bandwidth 
for business-critical use.
• Scalable broadband deployment for estates, campuses, commercial 
complexes and institutions.
• Managed network design, implementation, monitoring and responsive 
support.
• Security and performance optimisation — firewalls, intrusion detection 
and proactive management.
What you get   Dependable, high-performance connectivity from a licensed 
operator with managed support.
